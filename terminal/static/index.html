Keyboard

1–6Select a panel. Press again to make it full screen.
EscClose full screen, clear the filter, close this help.
/Filter the selected panel by name.
j kMove the row cursor down and up.
pPin or unpin the item on the cursor row (watchlist).
[ ]Change the view tab of the selected panel.
sSort by the next column. S reverses the sort.
cPrice chart layout: the list on the left, the chart on the right, a second panel under the list.
EnterChart the cursor row. In chart layout the chart follows the cursor.
1–6 · Shift+1–6Chart layout: pick the list. Shift picks the panel under it.
Tab · bChart layout: swap the list and the panel under it. b shows the next panel under it.
a · xHold the chart item as a compare line. Then move the cursor: the new item draws against it (% scale). x clears compare.
f · lFit the chart to all data. Log price scale on or off.
oOld fresh starts on or off: their average price at the same hours after launch, scaled to the live level.
rCopy changed files from G1 (read only), then reload.
?Show this help.

Money is gold (g), silver (s) and copper (c). "net" is after the 5% AH cut. Deposits are not included. BETA marks a beta realm value. Beta values never enter a live total, average or margin. Times are local G2 time.
